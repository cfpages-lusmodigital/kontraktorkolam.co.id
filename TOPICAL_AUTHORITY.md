# Topical Authority — KontraktorKolam.co.id

Last reviewed: 2026-07-23 (Asia/Jakarta)

## Role and boundary

KontraktorKolam.co.id should become the evidence-led Indonesian guide to commissioning, procuring, delivering, inspecting, renovating, handing over, and maintaining a swimming-pool project. Its primary job is to help owners and project teams manage contractor decisions and project outcomes.

`kolam.co.id` may independently explain the full pool knowledge universe, including water chemistry, equipment theory, pool history, sport, and user experience. Cross-domain overlap is allowed because Google treats the domains separately; however, links between them should exist only where the destination materially helps a reader. Within KontraktorKolam.co.id, every intent has one owner.

Educational content should live under `/panduan/` or another verified knowledge path. Existing design, construction, installation, mosaic, renovation, maintenance, treatment, plumbing, equipment, and service routes retain transactional intent. Location routes do not become evidence merely because the city name changes.

Safety boundary: this plan does not authorize unqualified structural, excavation, shoring, lifting, diving-envelope, waterproofing, gas/chemical, electrical, bonding, underwater-lighting, hydraulic suction, confined-space, hot-work, demolition, or public-health decisions. Final designs, methods, tests, and acceptance criteria require current official rules, licensed standards, manufacturer instructions, project calculations, and named qualified reviewers.

## Editorial and evidence gates

- Every article owns one intent, names its exclusions, and links to prerequisites and next decisions.
- Current official rules and licensed standards must be checked for the actual project and publication date; secondary summaries cannot establish compliance.
- Named structural/geotechnical, hydraulic, electrical, waterproofing/materials, public-health/water-treatment, landscape, safety, and legal reviewers are required where applicable.
- Original proof must identify project context, date, method, instrument/test source, limits, and permission to publish.
- Never invent company credentials, offices, local staff, projects, measurements, results, brands, stock, prices, warranties, regulatory approvals, or testimonials.
- Location content requires unique local evidence; changing a place name is never a publishable brief.
- Planning is comprehensive; publishing remains bounded and evidence-led.
